How It Works

From a photo to a clean street in 5 steps.

1️⃣

Snap & Upload

Take a photo of the waste on the Report page.

2️⃣

AI Classifies

The category is suggested; you can correct it.

3️⃣

Submit Details

Add name, phone and location, then get a Report ID.

4️⃣

Team Assigned

Your report is reviewed and assigned to a collection team.

5️⃣

Collected

Track live status until the waste is collected and completed.
